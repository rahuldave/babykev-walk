## The reference says what, and a guide says why

- The reference pages give the signature and the docstring of each function of `model.py`
- They do not say why the model packs a request, masks it, restarts the positions, and points
- A guide is prose that a person writes, beside the pages that the code makes
- smalldocs runs no code, so a page of the guide holds a grid as text

---

## Two pages on the model

- **The model**: packing, the mask, the positions that restart, pointing, and what trains
- **The mask**: the grid of a small request, beside the grid of a plain causal mask
- A name in backticks, such as `encode()`, links to its entry in the reference
- The grid of the mask is the picture of `test_mask_of_a_small_request`, so `just test` keeps it true
